💡 Safety Tips & Guidelines

🌙

Travel Safety at Night

Avoid isolated routes. Prefer well-lit, busy streets. Share your live location with a trusted person before travelling alone at night.

📱

Phone & Digital Safety

Keep your phone charged. Save emergency contacts. Enable location sharing and download offline maps. Use screen locks on all devices.

🚕

Cab & Transport Safety

Verify the cab number and driver before boarding. Share trip details with family. Sit in the back seat and avoid sharing personal info.

💪

Self-Defense Awareness

Learn basic self-defense techniques. Use your voice — shout "FIRE!" to attract attention. Carry a personal alarm or whistle when possible.

🏠

Home & Neighbourhood Safety

Keep doors locked. Know your neighbours. Have a code word with family. Don't open doors to strangers without verification.

🌐

Online Harassment

Report cyberstalking at cybercrime.gov.in. Don't share personal info publicly. Use privacy settings on all social media accounts.

🏋️

Public Spaces

Stay aware of your surroundings. Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, leave immediately. Walk confidently and assertively.

🤝

Know Your Rights

Every woman has the right to file a police complaint. The POCSO Act, Domestic Violence Act, and Section 354 IPC protect you legally.
